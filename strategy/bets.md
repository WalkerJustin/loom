## Active Strategy Bets

Every bet has an ID, a success signal, and a horizon. Projects trace to bets via `.loom/brief.md#serves_bets`.

**Format:**
- **ID** – B1, B2, B3, ... (used in project briefs)
- **Bet** – one sentence; what we're betting will be true
- **Horizon** – now (this quarter), next (1-2 quarters), later (3+ quarters)
- **Success signal** – observable, measurable, testable
- **Status** – active | exploring | dormant | retired

## How to update bets

1. **During ideation** – Spinner proposes new bets to explore
2. **Quarterly reviews** – Strategist scorecards each bet against outcomes (did it win?)
3. **At Decide time** – If changing a bet, Challenger reviews; Justin approves

**Never split a Decide-tier change** (e.g., a bet change) into smaller Review-tier items to avoid the Challenger gate.

---

## HARVEST feedback into strategy

HARVEST lessons flow back into strategy updates via:
1. Steward flags a pattern across multiple projects
2. Strategist analyzes HARVEST evidence
3. Strategist proposes a bet change or new bet
4. Challenger reviews (Decide tier)
5. Justin approves

Example: If 3 projects fail on the same assumption, that's evidence a bet should change.

## Golden thread (how strategy traces to work)

```
STRATEGY (vision + bets)

OBJECTIVES (quarterly OKRs per bet: O1.1, O1.2, O2.1, etc.)
  ↓
PORTFOLIO (which products/platforms deliver the OKRs: P1, P2, etc.)
  ↓
PROJECTS (each project in .loom/brief.md#serves_bets links to one or more bets)
  ↓
WORK (issues, PRs, milestones)
```

Any issue without a project, any project without an OKR, any OKR without a bet is an **orphan**.
