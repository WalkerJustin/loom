# LOOM Principles (draft v0.1)

1. **Humans own intent and gates; agents own throughput.**
2. **Artifacts over conversation.** If it isn't in the repo or an issue, it didn't happen.
3. **Inherit, don't copy.** Projects pin the kit and upgrade deliberately.
4. **Orchestrators route; specialists build.** No agent is both.
5. **Small, reversible steps.** One purpose per PR.
6. **Evidence at every gate.** Tests, evals and a clear "why."
7. **Every project teaches the warp.** Retros become canon or kit proposals.
8. **Ideas are alternatives, not edicts.** Canon is a living draft; propose better patterns openly.
9. **Gate by consequence, not by step.** Approval effort matches how hard a change is to undo and how far it reaches.
10. **A team of one still needs dissent.** Every foundational decision faces the Challenger first.
